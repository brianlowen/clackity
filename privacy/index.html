Flap Clock

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Flap Clock is made by one independent developer. It doesn't collect, store, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your approximate location (not your precise location) to show your local weather, sunrise and sunset. Your location is sent to Apple's WeatherKit service to get the forecast. It isn't sent anywhere else, and it isn't stored by the developer. Apple handles that request under Apple's privacy policy.

You can turn location off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services. The clock keeps working without it; it just won't show your local weather.

Notifications

If you allow them, the app schedules a "Timer done" notification on your iPhone for when a timer finishes while the app isn't on screen. It's created and delivered on your device; nothing is sent to the developer.

Data on your device

Your choices, like what each row shows and your typeface, are saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The app is paid for once through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; the developer never sees your payment details.

Children

The app doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
